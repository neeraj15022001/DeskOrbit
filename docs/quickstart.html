Two-Minute Setup

Quick Start Guide

Everything you need to set up DeskOrbit on macOS: display backlight control, WiZ v2 smart lighting, and on-device Apple Intelligence voice commands.

1

Installation & Requirements

macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or macOS 15.0+ (Sequoia) • Universal Binary (Apple Silicon & Intel)

DeskOrbit is designed with zero external dependencies and requires no background daemon or cloud accounts.

Option A: Install via Prebuilt .DMG (Recommended)

Download DeskOrbit.dmg from our latest release, open it, and drag the app into your /Applications directory.

Download DeskOrbit.dmg (v1.5.0)

Option B: Build from Source with SwiftPM

git clone https://github.com/neeraj15022001/DeskOrbit.git && cd DeskOrbit && ./bundle_app.sh
2

Granting System Permissions

Microphone and Speech Recognition for voice control

To enable the voice assistant and speech recognition, macOS will prompt for access on first launch:

Permission Usage Privacy Assurance
Microphone Audio capture for in-app Voice Assistant bar. Processed in real-time in memory. Never recorded or stored.
Speech Recognition Transcribes spoken phrases into text via SFSpeechRecognizer. Runs 100% on-device on Apple Silicon Neural Engine.
Accessibility Smooth multi-monitor brightness overlays and shortcut detection. Strictly local macOS window management.
3

Pairing WiZ v2 Smart Lights

Zero cloud • Local UDP port 38899 • HMAC-SHA256 authenticated

WiZ bulbs with modern firmware (1.38.0+) require cryptographic message signing. DeskOrbit automates this with zero manual key management:

📁 Method 1: Auto-Discovery

1. In the WiZ mobile app, tap Settings → Home → Export Settings.
2. Save or AirDrop homeStructure.json to your Mac's ~/Downloads folder.
3. Launch DeskOrbit. It auto-imports your 16-byte UDP key and Home ID instantly!

⚙️ Method 2: Manual Config

1. Open DeskOrbit Dashboard → Smart Lights.
2. Click Add Bulb.
3. Enter the local IP of your bulb (e.g. 192.168.1.2) and paste your 32-character hex key.

⚡ Dual-Band Router Self-Healing: If your Mac is on 5GHz and your bulb is on 2.4GHz, routers can drop ARP caches. DeskOrbit includes automatic synchronous ICMP ping probes that self-heal kernel routes on retry without dropping commands!
4

Voice Assistant & Siri AI

Local Apple Silicon Neural Engine • App Intents • Natural Language

No Apple HomeKit hub or bridge needed! Talk directly to DeskOrbit:

☀️ Circadian Daylight Sync

Say "Match light with daytime" or "Circadian rhythm". DeskOrbit computes astronomical solar elevation: cool 6000K daylight at noon, gentle 4800K in afternoon, warm 2200K amber at night.

🎬 Thematic Cinematic Presets

Say "Change color to theme of Money Heist" for deep crimson red, "Cyberpunk mode" for neon magenta, or "Matrix green" for terminal green.

🗣️ Siri App Intents

Say "Hey Siri, set light to 80% with DeskOrbit" or "Hey Siri, match lights with daytime in DeskOrbit" hands-free from anywhere on your Mac.

🔊 Named Audio & Display Control

Target specific devices: "Set MacBook speaker volume to 50%", "Set JBL volume to 80%", "Mute MacBook", or "Set display brightness to 70%". Handles spoken numbers ("fifty", "half") and relative levels.

🧠 Open-Ended Local LLM

Powered by Apple's on-device SystemLanguageModel on macOS Sequoia. Dispatches to audio, display, or light without cross-bleed.

5

Diagnostics & Real-Time Logs

Inspect socket datagrams and hardware telemetry

DeskOrbit outputs timestamped socket dispatch and hardware activity logs to /tmp/deskorbit.log:

tail -f /tmp/deskorbit.log

To test direct network connectivity to your bulb:

ping -c 3 192.168.1.2
